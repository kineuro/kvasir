## Why a CLA on an AGPL project

NILS is licensed AGPL-3.0-only. The AGPL alone would let anyone, including us, use your contribution under the AGPL and nothing else. The grant in section 2 lets the Project also distribute NILS, with your contribution in it, under other terms: that is how the Project can offer institutions a commercial license for hosted operation while the open edition stays AGPL for everyone, forever. The agreement does not take your copyright; you keep it, and you may do anything you like with your own work.

## How to sign

Post the following comment on your first pull request to this repository; the CLA workflow records your GitHub account, the date and the version of this document in the `cla-signatures` branch, and the signature covers all your later contributions:

```text
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

Contributions that touch only `contracts/` or `sdk/` (Apache-2.0) need no CLA; they carry a Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off instead, as [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) explains.
