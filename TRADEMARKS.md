# Trademarks

"NILS" and the NILS logo are trademarks of the NILS Project (kineuro, Experimental Neuroradiology Research at Karolinska Institutet). Registration of the word mark with the Swedish Intellectual Property Office (PRV) and the European Union Intellectual Property Office is in progress. The code is free and the name is not, and that is deliberate: the license lets anyone run, change and redistribute NILS, and the mark makes sure that what is called NILS is NILS.

## What you may do without asking

- Refer to the software by its name, truthfully: "built on NILS", "compatible with NILS", "a fork of NILS", "imports NILS registries".
- Keep the name in the file names, package names, commands and configuration keys that the software itself uses, including in a modified version, as long as the modified version is not itself called NILS.
- Redistribute an unmodified release under its name.
- Use the name in articles, talks, teaching, reviews and comparisons.

## What needs written permission

- Distributing a modified version under the name NILS, or under a name confusingly similar to it. Call your fork something else and say what it is a fork of.
- Naming a product, a service, a company, a domain or a package after the mark or with it: "NILS Cloud", "nils-pro", "nils.example.com", "@example/nils".
- Using the logo, or an altered logo, for anything but referring to the software.
- Anything that suggests endorsement by, or affiliation with, the Project.

Nominative use in the sense of the first list is always fine, and this policy is read in that spirit. Questions and requests: admin@kineuro.se.
